Privacy notice

How your information is handled.

The College Closet uses simple tools while the business is being validated. This notice explains what information may be collected when you buy an item, ask about one, or submit apparel for review.

Buyer inquiries

Product cards open a Google Form so you can ask about a listed item. The form may include the item name, school, size, condition, price, your name or handle, contact method, question, and purchase intent.

Seller intake

The seller page creates a text message to The College Closet. It may include your name, phone number, school, approximate pickup or shipping context, item count, condition, preferences, notes, and photos you choose to attach in the text thread.

Payments

Purchases are completed on a checkout page hosted by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. The College Closet never sees or stores your card number. Stripe shares only the order details, your email, and your shipping address with us so we can fulfill the purchase. Never send card numbers, bank details, passwords, student IDs, or exact dorm room numbers over DM, text, or forms — payment happens only through the Stripe checkout link.

Storage and deletion

Private buyer and seller details should stay in the approved Google Form response location or SMS thread, not in this public website repo. You can ask The College Closet to delete private details that are no longer needed for an active inquiry, transaction, support issue, or safety issue.
