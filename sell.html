What we accept

  • College hoodies, crewnecks, tees, long sleeves, hats, sweats, and shorts
  • Recognizable schools, teams, events, clubs, Greek life, or campus graphics
  • Nike, Champion, Adidas, Under Armour, Lululemon, New Balance, Colosseum, and vintage pieces
  • Clean, wearable items with no major stains, holes, or heavy wear

Skip these

  • Stained, ripped, or heavily worn items
  • Generic blanks with no school or team value
  • Counterfeit items or apparel you do not own
  • Socks, underwear, damaged shoes, or anything that cannot be resold safely
