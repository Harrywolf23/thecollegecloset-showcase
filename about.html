Our Mission

We help students shop school gear for less while keeping quality apparel in circulation. Listed items are reviewed before posting, and pickup, delivery, or shipping is confirmed after inquiry.

1. Collection

Clothing is donated to The College Closet.

2. Cleaning

Items are cleaned as needed and reviewed before listing.

3. Quality Check

Each item is inspected for quality before being listed.

4. Affordable Pricing

Items are priced fairly to be accessible to all.
